Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

← All games

This policy applies to all games published by AI Space Tails ("we", "us"), including Lane Keepers, Whoosh Sort and Dash Squad (the "Games"). It explains what information is collected when you play and how it is used.

Information we do not collect

The Games do not require an account. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other directly identifying information.

Game progress

Your progress, settings and in-game currency are stored locally on your device. If you delete the Game, this data is deleted too.

Information collected by third-party services

The Games may use the following third-party services. They may automatically collect device and usage information such as the advertising identifier, device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from IP address, app version and in-game events (for example, level started or completed).

  • Google AdMob — to show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch. How Google uses data.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — to understand how the Games are played so we can fix problems and improve difficulty and features. Firebase privacy.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — to process in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never receive your payment card details.

This information is used only to operate the Games, show and measure ads, process purchases, and analyse and improve the Games. We do not sell personal information.

Personalised ads and your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Games ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in the Game settings. On iOS, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Children

The Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and your rights

Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their own retention policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. To make a request, email us; because we do not hold identifying information, we may ask for details such as your device's advertising ID to locate the data.

Security

We rely on the security measures of Google, Apple and the services listed above. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Games change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
